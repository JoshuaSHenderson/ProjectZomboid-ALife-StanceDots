title=A-Life Stance Dots
description=[h1]A-Life Stance Dots[/h1]
description=Add-on for [b]Project A-Life [ALIFE NPCS][/b]. Puts a coloured dot over every A-Life NPC's head so you can tell friend from foe before they open fire.
description=
description=[h2]The dots[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Green[/b] — friendly. They will not attack you.
description=[*][b]Yellow[/b] — neutral. They leave you alone unless provoked.
description=[*][b]Red[/b] — hostile or wary. A-Life's combat code treats "careful" NPCs as hostile, so they get a red dot too.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How it decides[/h2]
description=It asks A-Life itself. The dot follows the same per-NPC, per-player stance the AI uses: admin overrides and personal grudges first, then the stance the spawn authored, then the faction's standing toward players, then the reputation you have earned by shooting at them. Attack a friendly faction and their dots turn red.
description=
description=[h2]Notes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Client-side only. No server changes, safe to add and remove mid-save.
description=[*]Dots hide for NPCs behind walls and past 30 tiles.
description=[*]Works in singleplayer and multiplayer.
description=[*]Dot size, height, opacity and range are constants at the top of the Lua file.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Requires Project A-Life [ALIFE NPCS]. Build 42 only.[/b]
