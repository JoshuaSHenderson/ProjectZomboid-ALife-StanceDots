title=A-Life Stance Dots
description=[h1]A-Life Stance Dots[/h1]
description=Add-on for [b]Project A-Life [ALIFE NPCS][/b]. Puts an icon over every A-Life NPC's head so you can tell friend from foe before they open fire.
description=
description=[h2]The icons[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Red crossed swords[/b] — hostile. They will attack you, or are attacking you or your people right now. Red means only this.
description=[*][b]Orange triangle[/b] — careful. Wary of you and won't shoot first, but don't push it.
description=[*][b]Yellow dot[/b] — neutral. They leave you alone unless provoked.
description=[*][b]Green dot[/b] — friendly. They will not attack you.
description=[*][b]Green shield[/b] — allied faction or crew.
description=[*][b]Blue star[/b] — companion: in your party, walking with you, or an allied crew following you.
description=[*][b]Blue house[/b] — lives at a base you manage (A-Life Jeem Extension residents).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How it decides[/h2]
description=It asks A-Life itself. The dot follows the same per-NPC, per-player stance the AI uses: admin overrides and personal grudges first, then the stance the spawn authored, then the faction's standing toward players, then the reputation you have earned by shooting at them. Attack a friendly faction and their icons turn red.
description=
description=[h2]Notes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Client-side only. No server changes, safe to add and remove mid-save.
description=[*]Icons hide for NPCs behind walls and past 30 tiles.
description=[*]Optional support for Project A-Life - Jeem Extension (residents, crew allies).
description=[*]Works in singleplayer and multiplayer.
description=[*]Sandbox options: dots or icons, and marker height.
description=[*]Icon size, opacity and range are constants at the top of the Lua file.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Requires Project A-Life [ALIFE NPCS] 1.4+. Build 42.21+ only.[/b]
